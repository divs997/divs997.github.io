## Short Biography
Divya Gupta is a senior at Carnegie Mellon University majoring in Artificial Intelligence.
She is completing a senior honors thesis in the Robotic Caregiving and Human Interaction (RCHI) Lab, advised by Prof. Zackory Erickson.
Her research focuses on machine learning for healthcare, particularly adapting time-series foundation models to forecast individualized cognitive trajectories in Alzheimer's disease.

## Full Biography
Divya Gupta is a senior at Carnegie Mellon University majoring in Artificial Intelligence.
She is completing a senior honors thesis in the Robotic Caregiving and Human Interaction (RCHI) Lab, advised by Prof. Zackory Erickson.
Her research focuses on machine learning for healthcare, particularly forecasting individualized cognitive trajectories in Alzheimer's disease.
She is broadly interested in robotics and in building machine learning systems for healthcare. Divya has previously interned at Amazon on the Alexa Skills team.
